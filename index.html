Our Story

Run by my father, grown as a family.

Golden Nest Poultry Farm started as one man's small coop and grew into a working egg and chicken farm through years of early mornings and hands-on care. It's still run the same way today — by family, for the local community.

Every bird is free to roam, every egg is collected by hand, and every order is packed the same day it's picked up or delivered. That's the whole philosophy: keep it honest, keep it fresh.
